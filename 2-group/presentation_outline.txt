SLIDE 8: Why the X-learner (core methodology slide)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- T-learner problem: the kit model is trained on only 20k members, so its
  noise dominates the uplift estimate
- X-learner (Kunzel et al., 2019), designed for unbalanced groups:
  1. Fit mu0 (no-kit, 980k members) and mu1 (kit, 20k members)
  2. Impute each person's effect using the OTHER group's model:
       kit members:    D1 = y - mu0(x)
       no-kit members: D0 = mu1(x) - y
  3. Regress D1 and D0 on x -> tau1, tau0
  4. Blend: tau(x) = e*tau0(x) + (1-e)*tau1(x), with e = 2%
     -> 98% of the weight on tau1, which relies on the precise mu0
- All base models: LightGBM with early stopping
